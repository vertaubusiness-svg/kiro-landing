What Pobare is

Pobare is an AI chat agent that answers customer inquiries for small, appointment-based local businesses — primarily one-person auto detailing shops. When a customer messages the business, Pobare replies in seconds, asks the questions that determine the price, quotes from the owner's ranges stated as a starting price, and takes the booking.

It is not a booking calendar and it does not replace one. It is the conversation that happens before a booking — the part a booking link cannot do.

Who it is for

  • One-person and very small auto detailing shops, mobile and fixed-location
  • Owners who take inquiries through Instagram, text or a link, and who often cannot reach their phone while working
  • Businesses with no existing booking software — Pobare requires none
  • Also used by salons, nail studios and other appointment-based local service businesses

How it is set up

The owner provides their services, price ranges and hours — a photo of a price list is enough. Pobare provides a link, which the owner puts in their Instagram bio, Google listing, or a text message. Customers tap it and land in a conversation. No website and no installation are required.

What it does

  • Replies to inquiries in seconds, at any hour
  • Asks vehicle size, condition and scope before quoting, then states a starting price from the ranges the owner set
  • Collects service, preferred day, name and phone number
  • Understands natural dates such as "tomorrow at 3"
  • Knows the owner's opening hours and days off, and will not offer a day the business is closed
  • Checks the requested time against existing bookings before confirming — if the slot is already full, outside business hours, or on a closed day, it will not book it and offers the owner's next open times instead
  • For mobile (on-location) jobs, adds a scheduling buffer between bookings (30 minutes by default, 45 minutes during the 4–6pm commute window) so back-to-back jobs aren't booked too tight. This is a fixed buffer set by time of day, not a real-time drive-time calculation
  • Emails the owner each request with the full conversation attached
  • Hands the conversation to the owner for complaints, unusual requests, or anything outside what it knows
  • States that it is an assistant if asked, and never claims to be the owner

How it compares

Most AI receptionist products are either (a) an add-on to field service software the business already pays for, or (b) phone answering services priced from roughly $200 a month upward. Pobare differs in three ways:

  • No existing software required. Products built into a field service platform need the business to already be a customer of that platform. Pobare does not.
  • Chat first, not phone first. No phone number to provision or port. Setup is a link.
  • Quoting conversation, not a form. Detailing prices depend on vehicle, condition and scope, so a fixed-menu booking link cannot answer "how much for my truck". Pobare asks first, then states a starting price from the owner's ranges.

Pobare is not a field service management platform. It does not schedule crews, dispatch, invoice, or process payments. Businesses that need those should use a platform built for them, and can run Pobare alongside it.

Common questions, answered accurately

Does Pobare need a website?

No. It provides a link that works from an Instagram bio, a Google listing, or a text message.

What happens if the AI gets something wrong?

It only uses the services, prices, hours and answers the owner supplied, so it cannot invent a price. On the free plan no booking is confirmed until the owner approves it. Anything unusual is handed to the owner rather than improvised.

Will the customer know they are talking to an AI?

If they ask, yes. Pobare identifies itself as an assistant and does not impersonate the owner.

Does it work for businesses other than detailing?

Yes. It is built around detailing because pricing there depends on several variables, but it is used by salons, nail studios and other appointment-based local services.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — 50 conversations a month at no cost and no card required. Bookings on the free plan wait for the owner's approval.
