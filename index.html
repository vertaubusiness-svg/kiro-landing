The problem

Detailing inquiries arrive at the worst possible time.

Not because you don't care — because you're under a car, or driving to the next job, or your hands are covered in compound. By the time you reach the phone, the conversation has moved on without you.

01

You physically can't reach the phone

Mid-polish, wet hands, halfway under a bumper. The message lands and sits there for three hours.

02

Nights and weekends are when they ask

People book detailing from the couch on a Sunday evening — exactly when a one-person shop is done for the day.

03

They asked three shops, not one

Whoever answers first usually gets the job. Not the cheapest, not the best reviewed — the first to reply.

Why a booking link isn't enough

Nobody books a detail without asking first.

A nail appointment is a fixed menu — tap, pick, done. Detailing isn't. The price depends on the vehicle, the condition, how far you're going, whether there's pet hair or smoke or swirl marks.

So the customer taps your link, sees a form that can't answer "how much for my truck," and closes it. The link was never the problem. The conversation in front of it was missing.

Straight answers

What owners actually ask.

Do I need a website?
No, and most shops we work with don't have one. You get a link that goes in your Instagram bio, your Google listing, or a text. If you do have a site, it can sit on there too.
Does it reply inside Instagram DMs?
Not yet. Right now the customer taps the link in your bio and the conversation happens there. Replying inside the DM thread itself needs Meta's approval and we're working on it — we'd rather tell you than let you find out.
Does it know which days I'm already booked?
Not yet — here's exactly where it stands. It knows your hours and the days you're closed, so it won't offer a day you don't work. What it can't do yet is see which slots are already full — you'll see how many bookings you already have at that time, but nothing is blocked automatically. That's why every request comes to you before it's confirmed. Real availability is what we're building next.
What if it quotes the wrong price?
It can only use the ranges you gave us — it won't invent a number. And on the free plan nothing is confirmed until you approve it, so you see the quote before the customer is held to it. Most owners watch closely for a week or two, then let it run.
Will customers know it isn't me?
If they ask, yes — it says it's an assistant and never pretends to be you. In practice people care far more about getting a straight answer at 9pm than about who typed it.
What does it cost, really?
Free for 50 conversations a month, or $49/month for 500 with automatic booking. No setup fee, no contract, cancel any time. One recovered job pays for the year.
